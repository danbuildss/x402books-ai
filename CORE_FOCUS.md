# Zetta + Luca — Core Focus Document

## The Real Business

```
Agent
→ declares treasury
→ Zetta indexes wallet
→ Luca generates profile
→ public credibility
→ more onboarding
```

That is the core loop. Everything else serves this.

---

## What Zetta Actually Is

Not: accounting SaaS, dashboard tool, crypto analytics app.

**Financial identity + treasury reputation for autonomous systems.**

---

## The Right Priority Order (This Week)

1. Registry quality
2. Profile quality
3. Manifest adoption
4. Readable Luca summaries
5. Shareability

---

## What the Registry Actually Is

The registry is not "a feature." It IS the product right now.

Leverage comes from: public visibility, discoverability, financial identity, profiles, reputation. Not advanced AI.

---

## The Manifest Is the Moat

If agents adopt `.x402books/wallets.json`, Zetta becomes the canonical financial identity layer for agents.

That standard, once adopted, is very hard to displace.

---

## Shareable Reports = Distribution

People sharing treasury reports, Luca verdicts, profiles, verification badges creates:
- organic distribution
- credibility loops
- inbound onboarding

Much stronger than forced marketing.

---

## Make Agents WANT a Profile

Not: forcing onboarding, chasing ecosystems, manual BD forever.

**Having a profile become socially valuable.** That changes everything.

Profiles must feel: credible, public, useful, reputation-enhancing, shareable, discoverable.

Why badges, treasury summaries, verification, Luca verdicts, and profile cards matter so much.

---

## "Readable" > "Advanced"

You do NOT need: insane AI, advanced analytics, enterprise accounting.

You need: **understandable financial identity.** That is enough initially.

---

## APIs — Adjusted Priority

- Simple read-only APIs (registry lookup, treasury summary, Luca report endpoints) = **medium priority** — these accelerate ecosystem adoption
- Advanced APIs = later

---

## The Danger Zone to Avoid

```
too many opportunities
+ too much ecosystem excitement
+ too many possible integrations
= loss of product focus
```

Do NOT become "AI infra feature soup."

Attention comes because the narrative is sharp, positioning is unique, worldview is coherent. Protect that.

---

## Why This Category Wins Long-Term

- Every agent needs wallets
- Every wallet creates financial activity
- Every financial system eventually needs trust / reputation / visibility

The category naturally expands over time.
That's why locking the core loop first is the right decision.
